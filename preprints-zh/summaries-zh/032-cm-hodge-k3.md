## Manuscript 1 ｜ 手稿一（核心）：The rational Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties

**Abstract（原文）**

> We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for complex abelian varieties with complex multiplication: every rational Hodge class on such a variety is a rational linear combination of algebraic cycle classes. As consequences, we obtain the generalized Hodge conjecture for CM abelian varieties, the Tate conjecture for abelian varieties over finite fields, and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in arbitrary characteristic.

**摘要（中译）**

> 证明具有复乘的复阿贝尔簇的有理 Hodge 猜想：此类簇上的每个有理 Hodge 类都是有理代数闭链类的线性组合。作为推论得到 CM 阿贝尔簇的广义 Hodge 猜想、有限域上阿贝尔簇的 Tate 猜想，以及任意特征下阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 标准猜想。

## Manuscript 2 ｜ 手稿二（核心）：The rational Hodge conjecture for products of K3 surfaces

**Abstract（原文）**

> We prove the rational Hodge conjecture for every finite product of projective complex K3 surfaces: every rational Hodge class is algebraic. The factors may be distinct or repeated, with no restrictions on their Picard numbers, periods, or endomorphism fields.

**摘要（中译）**

> 证明每个射影复 K3 曲面有限乘积的有理 Hodge 猜想：每个有理 Hodge 类都是代数的。因子可以不同或重复，对其 Picard 数、周期或自同态域无任何限制。

## Manuscript 3 ｜ 手稿三（核心）：Algebraicity of Kuga–Satake Correspondences for K3 Surfaces

**Abstract（原文）**

> We prove that the Kuga–Satake correspondence is algebraic for every smooth projective complex K3 surface. More precisely, the prescribed embedding of its transcendental cohomology into the cohomology of its Kuga–Satake abelian variety is induced by a rational algebraic cycle, with the fixed normalization and full even-Clifford target. The result also holds for isogenous Kuga–Satake models with the transported embedding.

**摘要（中译）**

> 证明每个光滑射影复 K3 曲面的 Kuga–Satake 对应都是代数的：其超越上同调到 Kuga–Satake 阿贝尔簇上同调的指定嵌入由一个有理代数闭链诱导（固定正规化与完整偶 Clifford 靶）。结论对带迁移嵌入的同源 Kuga–Satake 模型同样成立。

**链接**：[manuscript.pdf](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Algebraicity-of-Kuga-Satake-Correspondences-for-K3-Surfaces-October-3-2026/manuscript.pdf)

## Manuscript 4 ｜ 手稿四（核心）：Weil classes and Hodge classes on abelian powers

**Abstract（原文）**

> We prove the rational Hodge conjecture in every codimension on every self-power of a complex abelian sixfold with an imaginary-quadratic action and a compatible polarization whose rational homological Hermitian form is hyperbolic of signature $`(3,3)`$. We also prove it in every codimension on every self-power of a complex abelian variety of dimension at most five admitting an imaginary-quadratic action. Both results include nonsimple varieties and special periods with additional endomorphisms.

**摘要（中译）**

> 对具有虚二次作用与相容极化（有理同调 Hermite 型符号 $`(3,3)`$ 双曲）的复阿贝尔六重簇的每个自乘幂，在每个余维数证明有理 Hodge 猜想；对维数至多 5、可虚二次作用的复阿贝尔簇同样成立。两个结果都包括非单簇与带额外自同态的特殊周期。

**链接**：[paper.pdf](https://github.com/openai/math/blob/main/preprints/Weil-classes-and-Hodge-classes-on-abelian-powers-September-30-2026/paper.pdf)

## Main result ｜ 主结果（成果族陈述）

**Original.**

> Proves the rational Hodge conjecture for every complex CM abelian variety, in every dimension and codimension. Through Milne's theorems, this also gives the Tate conjecture for all abelian varieties over finite fields and the Hodge standard conjecture for abelian varieties in every characteristic. Companion results prove rational Hodge for arbitrary products of projective complex K3 surfaces and algebraicity of the Kuga–Satake correspondence for every such surface.

**中译。**

> 对每个复 CM（复乘）阿贝尔簇，在**每个维数与余维数**下证明有理 Hodge 猜想。经由 Milne 的定理，这同时给出有限域上所有阿贝尔簇的 Tate 猜想，以及每种特征下阿贝尔簇的 Hodge 标准猜想。配套结果对射影复 K3 曲面的任意乘积证明有理 Hodge 猜想，并证明每个此类曲面的 Kuga–Satake 对应的代数性。

## Background ｜ 背景与意义（编者注）

Hodge 猜想是千禧年大奖难题之一：断言复代数簇上每个（有理系数）Hodge 类都是代数闭链类的有理线性组合。阿贝尔簇是其最重要的试验场；CM 阿贝尔簇（自同态环足够大的一类）因结构刚性而成为最有希望突破的情形。K3 曲面与阿贝尔簇的联系由 Kuga–Satake 构造建立——把 K3 的超越上同调嵌入某个阿贝尔簇的上同调——因此「Kuga–Satake 对应是否代数」长期以来是把 K3 的 Hodge 猜想归约到阿贝尔簇的关键悬案。若本族成立，则：CM 阿贝尔簇情形完全解决；射影 K3 曲面的任意乘积情形解决；Kuga–Satake 对应对每个射影复 K3 曲面都是代数的。这些都是数十年的核心问题。
